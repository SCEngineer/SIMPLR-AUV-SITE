ORBITR

Low-cost autonomy, airborne.

Onboard Reasoning for Battlespace Intelligence, Targeting and Reconnaissance. ORBITR brings the Cognitive Vehicles ecosystem to Group 1/2 fixed-wing aircraft collecting RF/EW and video. The mission defines the aircraft: it is configured from a catalog of parts, flown in simulation before it is built, and assembled from a kit by the unit that flies it.

THE MISSION DEFINES THE AIRCRAFT

From order to aircraft.

Today an unmanned aircraft is bought first and fitted to missions afterward. ORBITR turns that around. Only a design that passes its mission in simulation is released to the unit to build.

THE FIRST MISSION

RF/EW and video, from the air.

The first ORBITR aircraft carries the same RF and video capability SIMPLR-AUV runs today, lifted to altitude where an antenna sees much farther.

RF/EW

Receive-Only Collection

Detects, identifies and geolocates emitters and maps the networks they form, transmitting nothing.

VIDEO

RF Cue, Video Proof

An RF detection becomes a reason to look: the aircraft orbits the emitter and points its camera at it, joining what it heard with what it saw.

EDGE

Decided on Board

Each aircraft collects everything and sends only what the plan allows: a short clip and a catalog of emitters, not a raw video stream. Links stay light and the aircraft stays quiet.

IN ITS ULTIMATE EXPRESSION

A team composed for the mission.

The end state is not one aircraft but a team built for each mission: aircraft and undersea vehicles, each configured and validated for its role, working together under one MAESTRO order and bringing back one fused picture.

STATUS
