THE PRODUCTS

The same products run every vehicle.

Each product does one job well, and none of them cares whether the vehicle swims or flies.

COMMAND

MAESTRO

Turns a commander's order into assignments across the fleet. Asks for outcomes, never dictates how, and launches nothing without approval.

PLANNING

COMPOSR

Plans each mission over real geography: route, sensor plan, communications plan and when to stay silent, and says plainly what it cannot do.

CONFIGURATION

COMPILR

Turns the mission into a validated vehicle definition from a catalog of parts with known performance, proven in simulation before it is built.

AUTONOMY

GIZMO

Onboard judgment: notices something, decides whether it is worth a closer look, investigates and resumes, recording the reason for every decision.

VALIDATION

The Simulated World

The vehicle's own flight software runs against a complete simulated world, fed by a separate computer exactly as real sensors would.

OPERATIONS

Operator Stations

Browser-delivered, role-based stations from tasking through analysis, the same for real missions and simulated ones.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Software-first, evidence-driven development.

The ecosystem runs end to end in simulation today: with SIMPLR-AUV, a commander's order becomes approved sorties, flown by the vehicle's own flight computers against a separate simulation computer, and fused into an intelligence report. Prototype vehicle hardware is in development, and vehicle-level performance figures remain design targets until controlled physical testing is complete.

ABOUT COGNITIVE VEHICLES

Autonomous systems built around mission reasoning.

Cognitive Vehicles LLC builds low-cost autonomy for low-cost, attritable systems: mission software, vehicle configuration, simulation, and the vehicles that carry them. SIMPLR-AUV is the company's first platform, an autonomous underwater vehicle for maritime reconnaissance. ORBITR brings the same ecosystem to small fixed-wing aircraft.
