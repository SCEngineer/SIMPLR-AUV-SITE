THE SIMPLR-AUV PLATFORM

Low Cost. Attritable. Built for the Mission.

SIMPLR-AUV is built to be sent where crewed platforms should not go. Its outer hull is 3-D printed and its electronics, sensors, and energy are commercial off-the-shelf, so vehicles can be built quickly, fielded in numbers, and risked on the mission rather than protected from it.

A free-flooding outer hull carries two watertight electronics cylinders and a wet-mounted, pressure-tolerant energy module, with common power, network, and mechanical interfaces between mission sensing, vehicle management, payloads, and energy.

THE FLAGSHIP MISSION

Find, then fix — with no one in the threat ring.

SIMPLR-AUV conducts maritime reconnaissance across three modalities at once. It surveys an objective wide and silent to find every contact of interest, the operator designates the ones that matter, and the vehicle returns to collect them in full for exploitation — all from an unmanned platform, with the crew kept well outside the objective.

RF

Passive RF Reconnaissance

Detects, identifies, and geolocates the radios, networks, and links at an objective from a single platform — receive-only, antenna awash, nothing radiated. Communications nets are mapped, their users counted, and the site's outside connections found.

ACOUSTIC

Acoustic and Sonar

Listens for running machinery and propulsion — including the low-signature semi-submersibles and narco-submarines that evade other sensors — and closes on what it hears with sonar to find, measure, and map the vessel and the structures around it.

VIDEO

Underwater Video

Closes on the vessel the sonar has found and images it pass by pass at close range, delivering visual confirmation fused with the RF and acoustic picture.

FORCE ASSESSMENT

Know who is there before you go in.

SIMPLR-AUV turns a reconnaissance sortie into an assessment the team can act on: how many are at the objective, how they are organized, where they were, what warning they have, and how they can call for help — delivered with the crew still well outside the threat ring.

WHO

Personnel on the Net

Identifies and counts the individual radio users at the objective, each tracked over the course of the sortie.

ORGANIZATION

Command Structure

Separates the networks they talk on, revealing how the group is organized and the infrastructure that ties it together.

WHERE

Positions Over Time

Shows where each user was each time they were heard, so posts, patrols, and movement stand out on the map.

WARNING

Early Warning

Locates remote sensors and alarms guarding the approaches, so the team knows what will see them coming.

REACH-BACK

Outside Connections

Finds the satellite and network links the site uses to report out or call for reinforcement.

TARGET

The Vessel Itself

Locates and images the vessel or object of interest, fused with everything heard around it into one product.

GIZMO MISSION ORCHESTRATION

Mission intent becomes adaptive behavior.

GIZMO evaluates mission objectives, operating zones, vehicle state, observations, and available capabilities to determine what the vehicle should do next. Its mission logic is deterministic, auditable, and subordinate to independent vehicle-safety functions.

DeterministicExplainableBoundedReplayable

COMPILR CONFIGURATION COMPILER

Mission intent becomes a validated vehicle definition.

COMPILR evaluates mission objectives and hard constraints, screens candidate configurations, solves mass and trim properties, ranks feasible options, and validates the selected configuration through the same simulation environment used by the autonomy stack.

The public site describes COMPILR's function and engineering role without exposing its internal scoring, implementation details, or private source.

THE SIMULATED WORLD

Every mission flown before it is launched.

SIMPLR-AUV comes with a complete simulated world. The vehicle's own flight software runs unchanged against it — the same code that goes in the water — fed sensor data so faithful the vehicle cannot tell the simulation from the sea. Missions are rehearsed, stressed, and scored against ground truth before a vehicle ever gets wet.

TERRAIN

Real Charts and Depths

Missions are built and flown over real nautical charts and seafloor depth data for the area of operations.

SIGNALS

A Living RF Environment

Radios, networks, sensors, and satellite links on the air the way a real site would put them there — for the vehicle to find.

SEA

Acoustics, Sonar, and Video

Machinery noise, sonar returns from vessels and piers, and camera imagery, all from the vehicle's true position.

VEHICLE

Navigation and Faults

Navigation sensors, GPS loss, sensor failures, and communications outages injected on purpose to prove the vehicle copes.

CAMPAIGNS

Scored Against Truth

Randomized scenarios run by the dozen, every result scored against what was really there — evidence, not anecdotes.

OPERATORS
