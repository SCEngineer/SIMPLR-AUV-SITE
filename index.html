THE SIMPLR-AUV PLATFORM

Modular. Capable. Built for the Mission.

SIMPLR-AUV uses a free-flooding outer hull with two watertight electronics compartments and a wet-mounted, pressure-tolerant energy module. The platform is designed for rapid payload integration, simplified logistics and reliable real-world operation.

  • Two-WTC vehicle architecture
  • Wet-mounted, pressure-tolerant energy module
  • Interchangeable mission payloads
  • Designed for rapid reconfiguration
  • Optimized for low-complexity operations

GIZMO MISSION ORCHESTRATION

Adaptive behavior. Bounded execution.

GIZMO turns mission intent, operating constraints and vehicle state into deterministic mission behavior while remaining subordinate to independent vehicle safety functions.

COMPILR CONFIGURATION COMPILER

Configure before you deploy.

COMPILR evaluates mission constraints, vehicle configuration, mass properties and simulation results to produce a feasible mission-ready configuration.

OPEN SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

Software-defined capability without locking the mission to one payload.

Common mechanical, electrical and software interfaces let mission capabilities evolve without rebuilding the entire vehicle architecture.
